Photos & privacy

Your memories stay on your device.
The decisions stay with you.

Mementide has no developer account, ads, or third-party analytics SDK. Media is handed to an iOS system service only when you explicitly share, save, favorite, or delete.

On-device by default

Photo comparison, previews, rescue processing, and video playback happen on your device.

Originals stay intact

When you save a result, Mementide creates a new copy instead of overwriting the original.

You confirm actions

Deletion starts only from an explicit action and continues through iOS system confirmation.
