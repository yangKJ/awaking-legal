How to revisit old photos without another task list

Make the entry small. Look at a few photos from your own life without deciding what to keep or delete. When a moment matters, favorite it or leave a sentence.

Mementide and 照惜 are the English and Chinese names of the same app. Available features and benefits depend on your installed version and purchase page.

Start with a short visit

Open Mementide Memory Stream to revisit allowed photos, Live Photos, and regular videos. Vertical scrolling only changes the current item; it does not change Photos. You can look for a moment and leave.

Use a date to reconnect

On This Day arranges photos from the same date across the years, including this year. Notice how a place, a person, or an ordinary part of life has changed. Not every photo needs to become a project.

Leave a sentence beside one photo

Write what happened then, or what you notice now. Notes stay in the app on this device. They do not modify Photos metadata, and Mementide does not provide cloud sync for them. Uninstalling removes local notes.

Decide when you feel ready

Favorite a moment, or preview a new look and save a separate copy if you like it. Compare similar photos together when useful. Prepared photo deletions still go through review and system confirmation. Revisiting and deciding can happen at different times.

If an original is only in iCloud, the system may need a network connection to retrieve it. Core comparison and look processing still happen on device.
