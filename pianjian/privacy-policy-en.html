Privacy Policy

Your photos and words stay on your device by default.

Effective and updated: September 8, 2026

In brief
Pianjian has no account requirement, ads, or third-party analytics SDKs. Ordinary creation does not upload photos, handwriting, messages, or drafts. The first free delivery uses an internet connection to verify an App Store identity and stores only an irreversible identity mapping, entitlement state, and delivery fingerprints. A processed main-image copy leaves the device only after you confirm AI Creation.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Pianjian iPhone and iPad app, and to the Pianjian website and support channels that you choose to visit.

2. Photo selection and on-device creation

Pianjian uses Apple’s system photo picker. It receives an item only after you choose a photo or Live Photo, and it does not require access to your full photo library. Thumbnails, layout, paper, edges, handwriting, recordings, and finished cards are processed on your device. If an original is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from Apple’s services according to your settings.

3. Collections and AI Creation

Collections reads published catalog data, bilingual copy, and official resources from a public content service. Browsing Collections does not upload your photos, messages, recipients, handwriting, recordings, or browsing activity, and it does not enable third-party analytics.

AI Creation is an explicit exception. After you choose a mood, Pianjian shows a confirmation. Only after you choose to proceed does it send a metadata-free processed copy of the current main image to Pianjian’s cloud service and a third-party AI image-generation service for that one generation. Canceling sends no image and uses no AI credit. Task input and generated results are removed after confirmed delivery, failure, or expiry; the server removes a result after you download and confirm it.

4. Drafts, handwriting, and messages

Photo copies, draft state, templates, paper choices, typed text, recipients, signatures, dates, places, handwriting, paid-delivery bindings, and finished-work records remain in the app sandbox. The service separately stores an irreversible App Store identity mapping, the first-delivery status, and project, source, revision, and finished-artifact fingerprints with idempotency identifiers. This prevents the same identity from receiving the offer again after reinstall; it does not contain ordinary-work files or provide draft or finished-artwork sync. Pianjian does not read Contacts or build a remote contact database.

5. Saving and sharing

Pianjian requests Add Photos permission only when you choose to save a finished card. It does not overwrite or delete your source photo. A card or template file leaves Pianjian only after you open the system share sheet and choose a destination; that destination handles the file under its own privacy policy.

6. Purchases

Apple StoreKit handles products, payment, Ask to Buy, verification, and refunds. Pianjian does not receive payment-card or payment-account details. The app retains the local product and verified-transaction information needed for delivery. The first-delivery benefit and AI credit packs reuse an irreversible mapping of the Apple-signed identity, while their entitlement state and balance remain separate. These records are not used for advertising, analytics, or cross-company tracking.

7. App Privacy label

Ordinary creation is on device. AI Creation transmits a processed main-image copy only after each confirmation and cleans it up after the task ends. We keep App Store Connect privacy answers current under Apple’s then-current definition of data collection; neither the label nor this policy treats “on device by default” as “no network transmission ever.”

8. Website and support email

When you visit this website, your browser connects to GitHub Pages. When you send a support email, your mail provider processes what you submit. Do not send original private photos, Apple Account credentials, full transaction records, or unredacted sensitive screenshots.

9. Your controls

  • Cancel the photo picker or an AI Creation confirmation;
  • Delete drafts and local works in Pianjian;
  • Turn off Photos, Add Photos, or Microphone permission in iOS Settings;
  • Cancel sharing or select only destinations you trust; and
  • Uninstall Pianjian to remove its app-sandbox data.

10. Updates and contact

If Pianjian later adds accounts, cloud sync, public submissions, analytics, advertising, or other developer data collection, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before enabling it.
