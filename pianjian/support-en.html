Support

A clear report helps your card continue.

Updated: September 7, 2026

I can’t import a photo

Open the system picker from Create or Replace Photo and choose a still photo or Live Photo again. If the original is in iCloud, connect to a network and wait for iOS to finish its download. Pianjian does not require access to your entire library.

AI Creation is unavailable, failed, or is still waiting

Check your connection and available AI credits, then choose a mood and confirm the request. Pianjian sends the processed main image only after you confirm. A failure or cancellation never replaces your original card; keep the draft and retry later.

My handwriting is missing from the card

Return to Write, make sure the active message source is Handwriting, then finish writing and return to the studio. Typed and handwritten content are both kept, but one card shows one active message source at a time.

I can’t save to Photos

In iOS Settings, allow Pianjian to Add Photos and make sure your device has storage available. Pianjian shows successful delivery only after Photos confirms the write; a failed save does not use a free delivery.

What happens when I cancel sharing?

Closing the system share sheet is not a successful delivery and does not delete your draft. You can continue editing or share again.

The price is unavailable or I can’t buy

Make sure the App Store is available and your Apple Account can make purchases, then try again later. Pianjian shows StoreKit’s local price and never substitutes a fixed amount. A canceled purchase does not use a free delivery.

Contact support

Please include your device model, iOS version, Pianjian version, steps to reproduce, and the message shown on screen. Redact private photos, Apple Account details, and payment information first.
